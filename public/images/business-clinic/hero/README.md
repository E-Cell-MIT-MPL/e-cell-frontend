The WebP copies are auto-oriented, grayscale, quality 88, and resized without enlargement to fit within 1500 × 1700 pixels. A stray top-edge border outside the symbol was excluded. Its geometry and proportions are preserved; no tracing, redrawing, or generative editing was used.
